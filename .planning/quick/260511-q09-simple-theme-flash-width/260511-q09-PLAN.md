<objective>
Fix simple-theme flash notice/alert messages so they expand to the full wrapper width.

Purpose: On the simple theme, `app/views/common/_menu.html.erb` renders `<div class="header">` with a right-floated `.email` `<li>`. Because `.header` did not contain its float (no BFC) and the inner `<ul class="navigation">` carried the user-agent default top/bottom margin, the float visually leaked below the 20px-tall header. The next sibling — `<div class="flash-message">` (display:flex) — therefore had its margin-box pushed left of the float, producing the narrow flash seen in `tmp/ss.png`. Modern/classic themes are unaffected because they don't render `<div class="header">` inside `.wrapper`.

Fix: add `display: flow-root` on `.header` so it establishes a block formatting context and contains the float; also reset the inner `<ul class="navigation">` margin to 0 so its natural height matches the header's fixed 20px line.
</objective>
