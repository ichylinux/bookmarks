## Overview

Add Mastodon OAuth 2.0 sign-in via a custom OmniAuth strategy with federated instance selection. Users enter their Mastodon instance domain on sign-in/sign-up, the strategy dynamically registers an OAuth app and resolves endpoints against that instance, and successful auth persists a composite uid (`instance_domain:account_id`) in `oauth_identities`. Mastodon appears as a fifth row in Connected Accounts preferences with disconnect support and last-auth-method safety guard.

## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**
- Custom `OmniAuth::Strategies::Mastodon` on `omniauth-oauth2` — existing Mastodon gems are OAuth 1.0 only
- Session keys: `mastodon_instance`, `mastodon_oauth_client_id`, `mastodon_oauth_client_secret`; stale credentials cleared on instance change
- Composite uid `instance_domain:account_id` in `oauth_identities` — one Mastodon account per user
- `MastodonInstanceNormalizer` rejects schemes, paths, IPs, and malformed hostnames before OAuth starts
- No live Mastodon OAuth round-trip in Cucumber CI — static presence check (Facebook precedent)
- Sign-in pages remain the only surface for linking Mastodon (IDNT-FUT-01 deferred to v2)
