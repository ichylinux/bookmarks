# Milestone v1.14: Landing Page Changelog

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-05-10
**Phases:** 46–48
**Total Plans:** 3

## Overview

Added a curated "What's New" changelog section to `/landing` for all visitors (guest and signed-in). Entries are defined in bilingual locale YAML (ja/en), rendered as dated, tagged, localized cards below the value grid, and backed by a helper method that sorts by date descending and caps at 10 entries.
